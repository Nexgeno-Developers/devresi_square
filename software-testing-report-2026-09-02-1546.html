5. Suggested next order

  1. Make Remember Me optional on public login.
  2. Scope landlord properties by workspace membership, not only created_by.
  3. Align the tenancy menu with a permission landlords actually have, or grant manage tenancies to Landlord.
  4. Strip staff-only fields from raise-repair when the actor is Tenant.
  5. Turn Debugbar off anywhere that is not a private local machine.
