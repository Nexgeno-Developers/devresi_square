15. Final Status

Final Status

Not Ready for Production

Recommendation

The staging path from signup through a property, a confirmed tenancy, and a rent invoice works. The tenant sees RENT-0001 and £1,250 outstanding. Landlord pages that a tenant should not open returned 403 or sent the tenant home. Mail, Chimnie lookup, and the Stripe trial checkout succeeded.

Fix before a production launch: the green “0 Compliance” pill while gas, EPC, and EICR are missing (B01); the invoice form’s blank amount and 12 Oct default due date (B02); blank tenant name fields (B03); and the phone-width dashboard clip (B04). Retest those four, plus share-certificate, the home appointment list, and the notification bell.

No critical security bypass was shown. The open high issues are enough to keep this host out of production. Staging copy (“Staging General Repair”, “user you”) should be cleared on any client-facing URL.

16. Testing Limitations

  • One Chromium browser. Firefox, Edge, Safari, and physical phones were not used. Phone layout was a 390×844 emulation only.
  • Performance numbers were not measured.
  • The database was not queried. Profile name mismatch is from the rendered form.
  • The invoice email and the tenant-invite email were not opened in a mailbox during this pass. The invite and the OTP were confirmed earlier in the session by the messages arriving and the product accepting them.
  • A tenant card payment was not completed. The landlord recorded no manual payment, so RENT-0001 is still unpaid on purpose.
  • Maintenance was not submitted with a photo, so no repair exists.
  • Password reset’s earlier HTTP 500 was not repeated after mail started working, beyond the successful 200 response.
  • rommankhan.nexgeno@gmail.com was not registered. Public signup creates a landlord, and the tenant seat was filled by the existing user.
