16. Testing Limitations

  • 188 of 214 functional inventory rows were not clicked. Contractor signup was opened only as a URL check. Super Admin was not used.
  • No rent invoice existed, so Stripe’s demo card was used for the subscription trial only, not for tenant rent.
  • Webhook signature, replay, expired OTP, resend throttle, and reset-token reuse were not exercised.
  • The database was not opened. Counts come from the screens.
  • Firefox, Edge, Safari, Android, iPhone, and a tablet were not available. The phone check was a 390px emulation in Chromium.
  • A login payload that looks like SQL was stopped by Mod_Security before the application. That is evidence the database was not queried with it, not a full injection suite.
  • Two test accounts remain on the host: the landlord trial and the tenant on 1 The Mall. They can be removed when this data is no longer needed.
