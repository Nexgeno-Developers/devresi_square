15. Final Status

Needs Fixes and Retesting

Signed-in tenant, landlord, and super-admin screens for the built inventory open and show the staging home, tenancy, invoice, repair, certificates, and operator tools. Landlord plan blocks on staff, roles, offers, SMTP, and plans hold.

Three high issues are still open: public £0 plans, login with no throttle, and compliance reminders that skip dates between milestones. Medium issues include raw HTML in tenant notices, test repair categories on the tenant form, missing security headers, and the estate owner being unable to open staff, roles, or SMTP.

Retest those defects after they are fixed. A production deploy is not recommended from this run. Card pay, payment return, archive, signup checkout, and password-reset email were not executed.

16. Testing Limitations

  • No production host was used. Port 8000 on this machine is a different application and was not part of this test.
  • Card payment, Stripe billing portal, signup OTP, and password-reset email were not submitted.
  • Archive, void, revoke, password change, and DSAR --force were not run.
  • A £1,250 invoice, RENT-0001, was created on the staging tenancy so the tenant invoice screens could be opened. The tenant confirmation was submitted and now shows Confirmed on 25 Sep 2026.
  • Bank-transfer details were not shown because no rent bank record was saved.
  • Correction-request submit, visit booking, document upload, and certificate share were not posted.
  • Firefox, Edge, Safari, and physical phones were not used.
  • Page-load timings were not measured, so performance is excluded from the score.
  • The first full PHPUnit run failed mostly because the isolated database had no roles. Those failures are not product defects. After the role seed, two tests remained failed: compliance reminders (BUG-003) and a stale rent empty-state string (BUG-007).
