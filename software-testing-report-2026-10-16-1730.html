15. Final Status

Not Ready for Production

Recommendation

Signup, the trial subscription, property setup, the tenant invite, and contractor profile creation work. Two business-critical defects remain: CRM mail and in-app delivery never leave Pending, and a completed test-card rent payment does not update the invoice. High issues also remain for tenant repair visibility, the split notification UI, tenant access to management screens, outbound mail authentication, and password-reset lookup. Retest those after the queue, the rent return URL, and the repair filter are fixed. Production deployment is not recommended while BUG-001 and BUG-002 are open. The two critical issues override the percentage.

16. Testing Limitations

  • Super Admin could not be opened. One seeder login failed. Contractor document review and job-marketplace events need that role.
  • Photo and PDF uploads could not be attached, so insurance, photo ID, shared documents, and compliance certificates were not submitted.
  • Scheduler events were not forced. Production artisan commands were not run.
  • A second card payment was not attempted after the first return URL failed, so the invoice was not paid twice.
  • Failed-card, bank record-payment, void, appointment create, and tenancy correction were not submitted.
  • No mobile or Safari pass. No performance timings. No direct database access.
  • Webhook logs were not visible. The rent result is what the tenant screen showed after Checkout.
  • Other contractors already on the host were not invited. The assignment save was tried only with the UAT contractor and did not persist.
